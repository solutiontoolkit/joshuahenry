Hello, I'm Joshua Henry, the developer behind STK-X (STK). I build web applications and data systems for people who need something that actually works in production, not just in a demo.

My day to day toolkit runs on HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Python, SQL and Power BI. I use them to build responsive front ends, Flask and Node back ends, and reporting tools that turn raw data into decisions people can act on.

Most of my recent work has come from small businesses and independent teams in Nigeria and beyond: news platforms, booking systems, and internal tools that needed to be built fast and maintained without drama. I keep the client in the loop from spec to launch.
